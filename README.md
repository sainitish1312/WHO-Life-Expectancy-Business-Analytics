# 📊 WHO Life Expectancy – Business Analytics

### Statistical Analysis, Predictive Analytics & Data Visualization

This project analyses global life expectancy data using a structured Business Analytics framework covering descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, and prescriptive analytics.

The analysis examines relationships between life expectancy, health indicators, socioeconomic factors, education, income, disease-related variables, and other country-level attributes.

> **Academic Team Project | Business Analytics | Team 6**

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The project applies multiple Business Analytics techniques to understand patterns and relationships within global life expectancy data.

The analysis covers four major analytical dimensions:

- Descriptive Analytics
- Diagnostic Analytics
- Predictive Analytics
- Prescriptive Analytics

The project also uses data visualization to communicate analytical findings.

---

## 🎯 Analytics Framework

### 1. Descriptive Analytics

The descriptive analysis summarizes the dataset and examines key statistical characteristics.

Techniques include:

- Summary statistics
- Mean
- Median
- Mode
- Standard deviation
- Kurtosis
- Skewness
- Minimum and maximum values
- Disease-related analysis

---

### 2. Diagnostic Analytics

The project uses correlation analysis and hypothesis testing to investigate relationships between variables.

Examples include:

- Income composition and schooling
- Adult mortality and alcohol-related variables
- BMI and Hepatitis B
- Vaccination rates between men and women
- Life expectancy between men and women

---

### 3. Predictive Analytics

The project applies regression analysis to examine how socioeconomic variables influence schooling.

The regression model uses:

**Dependent Variable**
- Schooling

**Independent Variables**
- Total expenditure
- Income composition of resources

The analysis evaluates the strength and significance of the relationships between these variables.

---

### 4. Forecasting

Forecasting techniques are used to estimate future life expectancy values.

The project develops forecasts for:

- Life expectancy of women
- Life expectancy of men

The presentation demonstrates forecasts extending through a 10-year period together with confidence bounds.

---
